Question 1 of 16

Fill in the Blank

Jim: If I'm elected, my number one priority will be __________ poverty.

Answer: alleviating

Teaching point

Alleviate poverty = reduce the seriousness or effects of poverty.

Useful collocations

  • alleviate poverty
  • alleviate suffering
  • alleviate the problem
Question 2 of 16

Fill in the Blank

A large portion of our country's population lives below the poverty __________.

Answer: line

Teaching point

Below the poverty line means living with an income below the minimum level considered necessary to meet basic needs.

Useful collocations

  • live below the poverty line
  • fall below the poverty line
  • cross the poverty line

Speaking

Discussion – Global Problems

Use the vocabulary and collocations from the lesson when answering the questions. Click each question to reveal a sample answer and teaching point.

Discussion 1 of 20

What do you think is the most serious global problem today? Why?

Sample answer

I think climate change is one of the most serious global problems because it can affect food production, water supplies, and people's health. It can also lead to more extreme weather events.

Teaching point

Use "one of the most serious + plural noun" when discussing major problems.

Discussion 2 of 20

What can governments do to alleviate poverty?

Sample answer

Governments can alleviate poverty by creating jobs, improving education, and providing effective welfare programs for people who need financial support.

Teaching point

Alleviate poverty = reduce the effects or seriousness of poverty.

Discussion 3 of 20

Why do some families live below the poverty line?

Sample answer

Some families live below the poverty line because they have low-paying jobs, limited access to education, or no stable employment. In some areas, there are simply not enough economic opportunities.

Teaching point

Live below the poverty line is a fixed and useful collocation.

Discussion 4 of 20

How can poverty affect children's lives?

Sample answer

Poverty can affect children's education, health, and future opportunities. In extreme situations, children may suffer from hunger or even become involved in child labor.

Teaching point

Use suffer from + problem/condition: suffer from hunger, suffer from poverty.

Discussion 5 of 20

Why are some people forced to live on the margins of society?

Sample answer

People may live on the margins of society because of poverty, unemployment, discrimination, homelessness, or a lack of education and social support.

Teaching point

Live on the margins of society describes social or economic exclusion.

Discussion 6 of 20

Do welfare programs really help alleviate poverty?

Sample answer

I think welfare programs can help people survive difficult periods, especially when they are unemployed. However, long-term solutions should also focus on education, employment, and economic opportunities.

Teaching point

Use "can help + object + verb": can help people survive.

Discussion 7 of 20

What should governments do to reduce dependence on fossil fuels?

Sample answer

Governments should invest in renewable energy such as solar and wind power. They can also encourage businesses and individuals to use cleaner forms of transportation.

Teaching point

Reduce dependence on + noun is a useful formal collocation.

Discussion 8 of 20

What are the advantages of renewable energy?

Sample answer

Renewable energy can reduce pollution and our dependence on fossil fuels. It can also provide a more sustainable source of energy for the future.

Teaching point

Renewable energy and fossil fuels are natural contrasting collocations.

Discussion 9 of 20

How does climate change affect ordinary people?

Sample answer

Climate change can affect people's lives through extreme temperatures, changing rainfall patterns, food shortages, and natural disasters. It can also increase the cost of food and water.

Teaching point

Use affect + person/thing directly: Climate change affects people.

Discussion 10 of 20

Do you think humans have caused irreparable damage to the planet?

Sample answer

I think humans have caused serious environmental damage, but I don't think every problem is necessarily irreparable. If governments and individuals take action, some of the damage can still be reduced.

Teaching point

Irreparable damage = damage that cannot be repaired or reversed.

Discussion 11 of 20

Why are some animal species endangered?

Sample answer

Many species are endangered because of habitat destruction, pollution, climate change, and illegal hunting. Human activity has had a major effect on wildlife.

Teaching point

Use endangered species rather than dangerous species. They have completely different meanings.

Discussion 12 of 20

What can individuals do to protect endangered species?

Sample answer

Individuals can support environmental organizations, avoid products that harm wildlife, reduce pollution, and learn more about endangered species.

Teaching point

Useful pattern: support + organization/cause.

Discussion 13 of 20

Why do food shortages happen?

Sample answer

Food shortages can happen because of droughts, floods, wars, poverty, climate change, or problems with agricultural production.

Teaching point

The same structure works with other shortages: food shortages, water shortages, energy shortages.

Discussion 14 of 20

How can climate change contribute to food shortages?

Sample answer

Climate change can cause droughts, floods, and changes in rainfall patterns. These problems can reduce agricultural production and eventually lead to food shortages.

Teaching point

Contribute to + noun means to be one of the causes of something.

Discussion 15 of 20

Should governments provide more homeless shelters?

Sample answer

I think homeless shelters can provide important short-term support. However, governments should also address the causes of homelessness, such as high housing costs, unemployment, and poverty.

Teaching point

Contrast short-term and long-term solutions with "However…"

Discussion 16 of 20

Why do some companies use child labor?

Sample answer

Some companies may use child labor because it is cheaper. In extremely poor communities, families may also feel that children need to work because they struggle to make ends meet.

Teaching point

Make ends meet = have enough money to pay for basic living expenses.

Discussion 17 of 20

What problems can people from disadvantaged backgrounds face?

Sample answer

People from disadvantaged backgrounds may have fewer opportunities in education and employment. They may also have greater difficulty accessing healthcare and other essential services.

Teaching point

Disadvantaged background is a common formal collocation in discussions about inequality.

Discussion 18 of 20

How can we reduce the gap between rich and poor?

Sample answer

We can reduce the gap by improving access to education, creating better employment opportunities, supporting low-income families, and making healthcare more accessible.

Teaching point

Useful collocation: reduce/narrow the gap between the rich and poor.

Discussion 19 of 20

Which global problem is most closely connected to other problems?

Sample answer

I think poverty is closely connected to many other problems. It can contribute to hunger, poor healthcare, child labor, homelessness, and even human trafficking.

Teaching point

Use be closely connected to when explaining relationships between problems.

Discussion 20 of 20

What can ordinary people do to help solve global problems?

Sample answer

Ordinary people can reduce waste, save energy, use public transportation, support environmental organizations, donate to reliable charities, and become more aware of global problems. Individual actions may seem small, but they can contribute to wider change.

Teaching point

Use contribute to + noun: Individual actions can contribute to positive change.
